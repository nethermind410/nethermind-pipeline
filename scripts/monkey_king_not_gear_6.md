## Script

**[COLD OPEN]**

Stop calling it Gear 6.

I said it in sixty seconds. Some of you argued. So now you're getting the long version.

Sit down. I love you. You're wrong.

That thing in chapter 1199 is not a gear. And by the end of this I'm going to tell you exactly what would make it one, and what I'll do if that day comes. It involves me saying sorry. To all of you. By name.

**[WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED]**

Quick catch-up for anyone who's been asleep or sensible.

Chapter 1198. Luffy goes full monkey. Not a bit monkey. Not monkey-ish. A giant black monkey with a cloud round his neck like a scarf his nan made him wear.

He beats the Rain God. Brilliant. Well done. Round of applause.

Chapter 1199. The Rain God's body is made of water. So when it goes down, all of that water comes down with it. On the village. He beat the rain and then gave everybody the rain. All of it. At once.

The giants hear his heartbeat. The Drums of Liberation. Except it sounds wrong. Same drum, different song.

And then he looks at Dorry and Brogy.

Dorry and Brogy. Little Garden. Two of the first people in this whole series to show him what honour looks like.

He rips a house out of the ground and throws it at them.

A house.

Dorry cuts it in half, because he's Dorry. And Luffy comes down out of the sky with a sword made of lightning in one hand and a shield made of cloud in the other.

Which is the exact picture on the wall in Elbaf. The old mural. Same pose. Same sword. Same shield.

So. Three reasons that is not a gear.

**[ONE — HE DIDN'T NAME IT]**

One. He didn't name it.

Think about who we're talking about. Luffy names everything. He named a punch after a pistol. He named another one after a bigger gun. He once named a move after a hawk because it sounded good. This man would name a sandwich.

And he doesn't just name them. He shouts them. Mid-fight. At the person he's hitting. It's rude, honestly.

That's what a gear is. I did a whole video on this. 2nd Gear, he built it himself, after Aokiji folded him, so he'd never lose anyone again. The gears were never a crown. They're tools. He made every one of them and he knows what every one of them costs.

Now look at this form.

No name. No shout. No pose. He beat his chest and then it was just happening.

That's not Luffy forgetting. Luffy doesn't forget to shout. That's Luffy not being the one in the room.

**[BUT YOU CALLED GEAR 5 A GEAR]**

And I can hear you. "You called Gear 5 a gear. That was a god waking up too."

Fair. Good point. Annoyingly good point.

But look at what he did on that rooftop. Kaido has just killed him. Actually killed him. A god wakes up inside his body. His hair goes white, his heart starts playing drums, the laws of physics pack their bags.

And what's the first thing Luffy does?

He names it.

He stands up, grinning like he's found a fiver, and he says this is my peak. This is Gear 5.

That's the tell. Even with a god climbing out of him, his first move was to stick a label on it and make it his. That's how you know he was still home.

In Elbaf, nothing. Not a word.

Same fruit. Same god. One of those times, Luffy was in the room.

**[TWO — THIS IS WHAT THE FRUIT DOES]**

Two. This is what the fruit does. And we were told.

Go back to Impel Down. The jailer beasts. Big animal guards, hit like trucks, get back up from anything. Awakened Zoans. And nobody home. All power, no person.

That's the warning. An awakened Zoan doesn't just make you stronger. The fruit has a personality. And if yours isn't loud enough, it takes the wheel.

Now add what we know about this fruit. It ran from the World Government for eight hundred years. Eight hundred. It has a will. It has opinions. It picked him.

So that's a fruit with a mind of its own. Sat inside a boy who has never once had a complicated thought. And we're all acting shocked that the fruit's got ideas.

Gear 5 was the god waking up. Fine. Lovely. Everyone had a great time.

This is the god getting out of bed.

**[THREE — IT'S OLDER THAN HIM]**

Three. It's older than him.

A gear is something new. Something he invented on the spot because the person in front of him wouldn't go down.

This isn't new. It's on a wall.

The giants have been arguing about that mural for longer than anyone can count. Their old book has three ways of reading Nika. Liberation. Laughter. And destruction. And nobody alive can read the original well enough to say which.

But every version agrees on one thing. When he turns up, the world changes. The powerful are scared of him. The people with nothing pray for him.

We got laughter in Wano. We've been promised liberation since Fish-Man Island.

Elbaf just got the third one.

So no. He didn't make this. He walked into a picture that was painted before his great-great-great-whoever was born, and it fit.

**[THE BIT I ACTUALLY CARE ABOUT]**

Right. Here's the bit I actually care about.

Forget the power scaling for a second.

Luffy does not hurt his friends. That's not a rule he follows. It's what he's made of. He will fight a god for someone he met on Tuesday. He declared war on the world for Robin and he didn't even stop eating.

So when he throws a house at Dorry and Brogy, that's not a power-up. That's the alarm going off.

In the 2nd Gear video I said the thing about him that matters. He hears the price. He understands the price. And he pays it anyway, eyes open. That's not a victim. That's a man deciding.

This is the first time in twelve hundred chapters he isn't deciding.

And that's why the name matters to me. Call it Gear 6 and you're giving him credit for a choice he didn't make. You're saying he did this.

He didn't do this. This is being done to him.

**[SO WHAT IS IT]**

So what is it?

It's Nika with the handbrake off, and Luffy in the passenger seat. Still in the car. Not driving.

And here's why I'm not actually worried. Go back to the oldest rule this fruit has. He's rubber because he thought he was rubber. The body is only limited by his imagination.

Which means he's a monkey god right up until he decides he's Luffy.

That's the way back. Not a punch. Not a bigger form. Him remembering who he is.

And if you want my actual bet on what does it?

Sanji. And a plate.

I'm not even joking. This series has fixed bigger problems with meat. Fifth Gear runs on lunch. I'll be amazed if the monkey doesn't.

**[THE WORST POSSIBLE WEEK FOR IT]**

And the timing. Honestly.

Because while all this is going on, Imu is in Elbaf. The actual ruler of the world. In the last chapter Imu told Loki to hand over the island's ancient technology. Loki said he hasn't got a clue what that is. So Imu decided to just take the whole island and look for it personally.

That's the situation. The thing that's run the world for eight hundred years has turned up to rob the giants.

And the one person who can stop it is outside. As a monkey. Throwing a house at his friends.

You could not pick a worse week to lose the wheel.

Which is exactly why I think he gets it back. This series doesn't put Luffy in the passenger seat right as the final boss walks in for no reason. The whole point is watching him climb back over into the front.

**[WHEN I'LL CALL IT GEAR 6]**

So here's my deal with you lot.

The day he does this on purpose. The day he goes monkey because he chose to, gives it a daft name, and shouts it at somebody's face.

That's a gear. I'll call it Gear 6. I'll say sorry in the comments. To every one of you. By name. It'll take me a week.

Until then, it's not his. And I'm not handing it to him.

**[THE WAR]**

Your turn.

Who's driving right now? Luffy, Nika, or is it a team effort?

Tell me I'm wrong. Scrap it out in the comments. Be nice to each other, you're all I've got.

Chapter 1200 is Sunday, and Robin's about to read an eight-hundred-year-old letter while her captain's outside throwing houses. So that'll be calm.

And if Sunday proves me wrong about any of this, good. I'll be back here saying so. That's the deal.

The 2nd Gear video is on the channel if you want to see what a real gear costs. Subscribe if you're staying for the next one.

Done.
