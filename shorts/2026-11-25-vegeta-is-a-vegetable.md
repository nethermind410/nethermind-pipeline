## Facts, each from a page that was opened (25 Nov 2026)

| Line | Claim | Page | What it says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vegeta's name is the word vegetable | Wikipedia, "Vegeta" | "Vegeta's name is a pun on the word vegetable itself." |
| 2 | Saiyans are named after vegetables; Saiya is yasai rearranged | Wikipedia, "List of Dragon Ball characters" and "Goku" | "the Saiyans are named after vegetables"; "Saiya", an anagram for Yasai, lit. vegetable. |
| 3 | Kakarot is carrot | Wikipedia, "Goku" | "An English to Japanese play on the word 'carrot'." |
| 5 | Bulma's family is named after underwear; Bulma is bloomers | Wikipedia, "List of Dragon Ball characters" and "Goku" | "Bulma and her family are named after undergarments"; Viz renamed chapter 1 "Bloomers and the Monkey King". |
| 6 | Vegeta and Bulma have a son, Trunks, and a daughter, Bra | Wikipedia, "Vegeta" and "List of Dragon Ball characters" | "has a son named Trunks with Bulma"; "a younger child daughter named Bra, known as Bulla in the English dub". |
| 7 | Frieza is freezer; the Ginyu Force are named after things kept in a fridge | Wikipedia, "List of Dragon Ball characters" | "because Frieza is a pun on freezer, the Ginyu Force are named after products that are kept in a refrigerator." |

Wikipedia gives Akira Toriyama's own book, Dragon Ball Tenkaichi Densetsu (Shueisha, 2004), as its source for these.
That book was not opened. Line 7 says Frieza "blew up their planet": Wikipedia's Goku page says his homeworld was
destroyed "at the hands of Frieza". Line 6 uses "Bra", her Japanese name; the English dub says Bulla.

Lines 4 and 8 are jokes and an opinion, not facts.
