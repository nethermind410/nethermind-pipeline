# Daily Short, 25 Nov 2026: Dragon Ball Z is a shopping list

Status: **DRAFT, waiting on Courtney's approval** of the script and storyboard. Nothing is built or voiced until he
says yes.

Written by Claude Code on his word (25 Nov): "I want you to do the daily, like, short, so you sort of make the script,
all that sort of stuff. So I need one for today. Funny ... making it better than what the fuck it has been."

## Why this one

Built on what `results/VIEWS-DROP.md` found worked: one surprising fact, said plainly, a big question on screen in the
first second, a subject everyone knows, standing on its own with no pointer to a long-form. Dragon Ball has not been
done on the channel. And it is funny without needing a joke written for it: the facts are the joke.

## His notes on the first draft (25 Nov)

- The title should tie it to real life: "every character can be picked up off a shopping list or something."
- "the script is all right, but then sort of add that sort of comical value into it ... what is funny about them sort
  of being these vegetables and like how strong they are ... and then at the end it's like, so always eat your veggies"

- On the ending: "because you'll never know when a carrot comes in handy. You know, got to play on words and be a bit
  of a pun." His ending line is used as he said it.

- On draft 3: "this for me just reads like statements ... like I'm reading fucking bullet points, but I like sort of the
  vibe ... finesse it, finalize it, and just tighten it up".

Draft 4 below is one run of talk, not a list: each line hands over to the next ("Start with", "And it's not only
him, because", "Which makes", "Then", "Until", "So"). Same facts, his ending as he said it.

| Line | Claim | Page | What it says |

| 2 | Vegeta is proud | Wikipedia, "Vegeta" | "is full of his Saiyan pride ... extremely haughty". |
| 3 | The Saiyans are a warrior race | Wikipedia, "Vegeta" | "warrior race known as the Saiyans". |
| 6 | Frieza destroyed the Saiyans' planet and nearly the whole race | Wikipedia, "Goku" | "the galactic tyrant Frieza, who destroyed the Saiyans' home planet and nearly the entire race". |
| 7 | Goku goes Super Saiyan (blond) fighting Frieza, and Frieza is defeated | Wikipedia, "Goku" and "List of Dragon Ball characters" | "During his epic battle with Frieza, Goku becomes the first Saiyan in a thousand years to transform"; "the Super Saiyan form having blonde hair"; "After Frieza's defeat". |

Wikipedia gives Akira Toriyama's own book, Dragon Ball Tenkaichi Densetsu (Shueisha, 2004), as its source for these.
That book was not opened. Line 5 uses "Bra", her Japanese name; the English dub says Bulla. Line 7 is the one soft
spot: the pages say Goku fought Frieza and that Frieza was defeated, not in one sentence that Goku beat him.

"Salad", "put on ice", "roasted" and the ending are jokes and puns, not facts. The spoken "Bulma means bloomers"
was cut for flow; BULMA to BLOOMERS still shows on screen.
