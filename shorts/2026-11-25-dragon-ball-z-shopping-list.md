# Daily Short, 25 Nov 2026: Dragon Ball Z is a shopping list

Status: **APPROVED by Courtney, 25 Nov 2026. With Muse for voice, build and Private upload.**

Written by Claude Code on his word (25 Nov): "I want you to do the daily, like, short, so you sort of make the script,
all that sort of stuff. So I need one for today. Funny ... making it better than what the fuck it has been."

## Why this one

Built on what `results/VIEWS-DROP.md` found worked: one surprising fact, said plainly, a big question on screen in the
first second, a subject everyone knows, standing on its own with no pointer to a long-form. Dragon Ball has not been
done on the channel. And it is funny without needing a joke written for it: the facts are the joke.

## On screen in the first second

`IS DRAGON BALL Z JUST A SHOPPING LIST?`

## Script: FINAL, approved by Courtney 25 Nov

These are his own words. He rewrote the script himself and asked for it to be made "a bit more seamless" "without
changing the script tooooo much". The agreed changes were: "Prince of all Saiyans" (the character's real title), "the
freezer" as the spoken form of the Frieza pun, "with Bulma" added to the cupboard line, and his line "and they all go
on before bulmas" dropped ("That's fine"). He asked for "Which used to mean something entirely different" to stay in.
**Voice these lines character-exact.**

1. Dragon Ball Z's biggest names are on your shopping list. Come walk the aisles.
2. Start with Vegeta. Prince of all Saiyans, proudest man in anime, and his name is just the word vegetable. Which used to mean something entirely different.
3. His whole race, the Saiyans, is a play on yasai, which is, you didn't guess it, vegetables. Food for thought.
4. Carrots used to make me think I could see in the dark. Now all I see is Kakarot yelling at the freezer. Little fucker couldn't even put rabbit food on ice.
5. And what happens when you throw a vegetable in a cupboard with Bulma? Trunks and Bra, that's what. That's a whole outfit and lunch.
6. So never turn your nose up at a salad, and always be dressed for the occasion. Carrots are tough.

134 words. About 50 seconds at the pace of his 100 BRICKS read. The voice does not state every name
origin outright; the on-screen name swaps carry the facts.
