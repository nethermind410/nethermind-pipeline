# Daily Short, 27 Nov 2026: Naruto's dad is getting an anime, and fans are making it

Status: **DRAFT, waiting on Courtney.** Nothing goes to Muse until he approves or rewrites it.

Written by Claude Code on his word (27 Nov): "Where's the script? Don't we do the scripts here and then you send it to
Muse?" Muse's slate had this as "Minato news Short ... the adaptation is fan-made; frame it as a fan project".

## Why this one

One surprising fact, said plainly, on a subject everyone knows (`results/VIEWS-DROP.md`): the Minato anime is not
being made by the studio. Fans voted him number one, Kishimoto drew him a one-shot, and a non-profit fan studio is
animating it. It stands alone, no pointer to a long-form. The channel record ("One Piece Was Supposed to End in 2002",
1,010 views in 13 hours) is this same shape.

## CHECK BEFORE VOICING: is it out yet?

Every page that opened is from November 2024. As of those pages there was a trailer (3 March 2024), no release date,
and the studio said it was still working. **Nobody has confirmed where it stands today.** Two searches on 27 Nov 2026
found no release report; one unopened social post from about April 2026 calls it "nearing completion". The script
below is written so every line is true whether or not it has come out. Before voicing, someone who can open Galactic
Republic Studio's own X or YouTube page reads the latest post. If it has been released, line 1 changes to say so and
he gets asked.

## On screen in the first second

`WHO IS MAKING THE MINATO ANIME?`

## Script: DRAFT in his voice (`shorts/VOICE.md`)

His to rough up. If he rewrites a line, his words are used character-exact.

1. Naruto's dad is getting his own anime. And Studio Pierrot isn't even involved.
2. It started with a vote. The whole cast, the whole world picking a favourite, and first place went to his dad. Not Naruto. In Naruto.
3. So Kishimoto drew Minato his own one-off. He saves Kushina, he invents the Rasengan. Hell of a first date.
4. Then a non-profit mob of fans called Galactic Republic stuck their hands up and said, we'll animate that.
5. I can't get three friends to agree on dinner. These legends are animating a whole Naruto prequel and not making a fucking cent.
6. So vote in everything, and if your old man's more popular than you, cop it. Believe it.

113 words, about 40 seconds at his pace. One thing from his own life (line 5), one wink (line 3), one swear, a useless
moral and a two-word button. No banned words, no em dashes.
