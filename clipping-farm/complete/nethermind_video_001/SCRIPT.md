# NETHERMIND — VIDEO 001

## Working Title
Why Does East Asian Internet Look "Wrong"?

## Format
YouTube Short / 9:16 / ~55 seconds

## VOICEOVER

Why does East Asian internet look so fucking weird to Western eyes?

Look at this.

Dense. Busy. Tiny text. Buttons everywhere.

And the obvious assumption is that the technology is just... behind.

But that's actually the interesting part.

Some of the most visited websites in South Korea today still look like this.

Why?

It's culture.

We don't experience design objectively.

We learn what information is supposed to look like.

What feels clean to one person can feel empty, confusing, or even suspicious to someone from somewhere else.

So maybe the weird website isn't the weird thing.

Maybe it's your definition of normal.

## DELIVERY

Fast, conversational, slightly incredulous.

Do NOT sound like a documentary narrator.

Pause after:

"But that's actually the interesting part."

"Why?"

"Maybe it's your definition of normal."
