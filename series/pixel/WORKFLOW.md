# The pixel workflow — as the twelve scripts show it

Written by Claude Code, 23 Nov 2026, from `SCRIPTS.md` only. Every line
below is something those scripts show. Courtney corrects anything wrong.

## The episode, line by line

Every episode is five or six spoken lines, about 100 words.

1. **The vote's result, as "you".** "You wore the suit. Now your shadow has
   company." The viewer is the one in the Nether.
2. **How the comments went.** "The comments split, so you took the darker
   deal."
3. **One new thing appears,** growing out of the last choice. The suit
   becomes the shadow; the shadow takes the wheel; the wheel hits the wall.
4. **The real lore, named out loud with its source.** "In Jujutsu Kaisen,
   that's a Binding Vow." Early episodes add "And it's real lore."
5. **The price, and one meter.** "Nether-mark: 40 percent." One number, said
   once.
6. **A or B, in full,** then "Comment A or B. Part N follows the vote."

## The rules underneath

- **One story.** Each episode starts from the last vote and nothing else.
- **One lore mechanic per episode,** and the mechanic is the dilemma.
- **One meter.** No second ledger, no inventory.
- **Written one at a time.** The next script is written after the vote, not
  planned twelve ahead. The dates show roughly one a day.
- **Second person, present tense, short sentences.**
- **Delivery tags on each line for the voice:** `[cold, fast]`,
  `[slowly, hushed]`, `[pause]` before the choice.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, voice `f3AcTizLlkmZHnO1dCyX`.
- **One line is one picture.** Five or six lines, five or six shots.
