## The rules

1. A normal choice costs 5 to 15 bricks.
2. A greedy pick pays a little now and costs about three times that later, and the cost lands at the open of a later
   part where the comments can see it.
3. Nothing is free: the option that looks safe still costs.
4. Parts 1 to 5 cannot kill him on any path. **From Part 6 on he can reach zero, turn to stone, and the series ends
   there.**
5. Each part opens on the last part's close, exactly.

## Can he die early?

- Parts 1 to 5, worst votes every time: 100, 104, 82, 75, 65, 55. He reaches Part 6 alive on every path.
- From Part 6, worst votes every time: 55, 40, 30, 22, 13, then Part 10 takes 18 or 22 and he is stone in Part 10.
  A kind run with the Clock's +12 gets him to the Door. So the votes decide it.
- When a count would hit zero, the statue ending in `ARC.md` section 6 plays in that part and becomes the finale.
