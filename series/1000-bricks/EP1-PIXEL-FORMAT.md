## Script (99 words)

[cold, slowly] You wake up on a stone floor. You're made of bricks. A thousand of them.
[flat] The number glows on your chest. Down the corridor stand statues. That's what zero looks like.
[slowly, hushed] One statue's arm hangs loose. Red stone. Still warm.
In Hellboy, a stone right hand is the Right Hand of Doom: the key to the one door that should stay shut.
[rising] Take it and you're thirty-five bricks richer. The statue's eyes open. Bricks: one thousand.
[pause] A: Take the arm. Or B: Leave it, and pay fifteen to walk on. Comment A or B. Part 2 follows the vote.
