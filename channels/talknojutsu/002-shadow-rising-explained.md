## 6. Shown versus guessed, in one place

**Officially shown or stated (official Naruto site, Jump Festa site, Variety):**
- A new Naruto manga by Masashi Kishimoto, titled Shadow Rising, drawn entirely by him.
- A teaser at the end of the New York Comic Con stage on 10 October 2026.
- Six images: six past Hokage faces plus a seventh under construction; Naruto holding up the Hokage hat; Kakashi smiling; Sasuke standing; Sakura looking at a baby; Naruto and Sasuke clashing.
- More details at the Naruto Jump Super Stage, Jump Festa 2027, 19 to 20 December 2026.

**Not announced on any page I opened:** when it is set, format and length, magazine or app, release date, English release, the baby's name, why Naruto and Sasuke clash.

**Outlet guesses (say who said it):**
- "Set after the events of the Fourth Great Ninja War" and "Naruto had not become the Hokage yet but was getting rather close" (Game Rant, 23 November 2026).
- Both projects "expected to be released in 2027" (Game Rant).
- The live-action film "will likely cover the first few arcs" (Game Rant, which itself says there is no official statement).

**This video's own guesses:** the era, the baby, the span of years, the fight, the title. All marked MY READ on screen.

## 11. What I could not verify

- **The teaser itself.** I did not watch it. Every description of what it shows comes from the official site's written round-up. Before a storyboard is drawn, someone has to watch the teaser and confirm the six images, their order, and that the seventh face reads as Naruto's.

- **"Sketches" versus "storyboards".** The official English article says "storyboards from the manga" and warns that some of its articles are machine translated. The Japanese article was not opened, so the exact Japanese word is unknown.
- **Hiroyuki Sanada as the Third Hokage.** One outlet (Game Rant) citing Deadline. Deadline was not opened. A web search turned up no second report, only older fan casting pieces. Keep it to one attributed line, or cut it.
- **Charles Melton as Kakashi.** Game Rant only.
- **The 18 November "new message" from Kishimoto.** The Viz page was not opened. The text matches the comment already read out on 10 October, so it should not be presented as new.
- **Project Rasengan as a "three-year" plan.** Wiki only.
- **English spellings of the crossover staff.** Read from Japanese credits by me.
- **The time conversion to Australian time,** and where the livestream can be watched from Australia.
- **Shueisha and Viz statements about format or English release.** None found. Viz's site was not opened; a search found nothing from Viz about Shadow Rising.
- **Other outlets' claims seen only in search results** (a "prequel", "immediately after chapter 699", a Kurama reunion). Not opened, so not used.
