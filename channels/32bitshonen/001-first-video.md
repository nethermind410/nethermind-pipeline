## Which video, and why

**Picked: (a), narrowed to "Every PS2 Naruto Ultimate Ninja game ranked". Five games, one console, one studio.**

- Checkable: each game has a release date per region, a publisher, and review pages. (b) "ahead of their time" is opinion with nothing to check. (c) cancelled games run on rumours and forum posts, with almost no footage.
- Footage: all five can be captured from discs. PAL discs suit an Australian.
- Clear promise, with a built-in hook: the fifth game never came out in North America but did reach Europe and Australia.
- It sits between the Naruto channel and the gaming channel.
- Five games at about 100 seconds each fits 10 minutes.
